Engine settings

AdSpark runs fully offline with its built-in template engine. Add your own API key to switch the engine to real AI. Your key never leaves this browser — it's stored locally and sent straight to the provider.

What's what?
Create tab — the brief form

Type your product, audience and biggest benefit. Pick a tone and platforms. One click gives you ready, platform-native ad copy for each one.

Templates vs AI mode

Templates (gold dot) is a built-in writing engine — works fully offline, no key needed.
AI mode (orange pulsing dot) sends your brief to an LLM through your own API key (gear icon) for fresher, less repetitive copy. If AI fails or errors, AdSpark silently falls back to templates.

A/B pair mode

Toggle it on before generating. Each card gets a "Save as A/B pair" button: it saves your edited version as arm A and a re-rolled spin as arm B. Run both arms live in a real campaign, then in the Library hit 👑 Crown winner on the one that performed. The engine records the winning tone and starts preferrring it in future generations.

Performance budget score

Every generated card gets a 0–100 copywriting health score. It checks: filler words ("very", "really", "amazing"), heavy adverbs, sentences longer than ~24 words, ALL-CAPS shouting and exclamation overload. Sharp = ship it. Okay = tighten. Needs work = rewrite before spending money on it.

Rivals tab

Competitive strategy tool. Add your product + your unfair advantage, then up to 2 competitors and what they're known for. You get a head-to-head positioning table, attack angles rivals structurally can't copy, a do/don't list and platform advice. Add an AI key for sharper, market-aware analysis.

Library

Everything you saved lives here. ★ marks personal winners, 👑 crowns A/B champions. Losers fade out. This is your "what actually works" archive.
